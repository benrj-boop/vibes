Hey! Sharing something I built for my team's weekly standup that's been working really well — in case anyone wants to steal it.

**The idea:** Most standups are all work, no connection. Ours combines vibe checks (how are you feeling? what are you proud of? what are you looking forward to?) with actual work discussion — blockers, craft topics, things that need group air time. Human first, then work.

**The twist:** It's disguised as a retro RPG. You walk a pixel character around a map and bump into NPCs to answer your check-in prompts. Sounds silly, works surprisingly well — people open up way more in a playful context than a form.

In our first session, we learned about a super interesting hobby one of our teammates has that nobody knew about. That's the kind of thing that changes how a team works together.

There's also a classic mode (clean form-based UI) if games aren't your team's vibe.

**Links:**
- Classic mode: https://benrj-boop.github.io/vibes/
- Adventure mode: https://benrj-boop.github.io/vibes/game.html
- Repo (fork + customize in ~10 min): https://github.com/benrj-boop/vibes

Just swap in your team names and optionally connect a Google Sheet. Setup instructions are in the README.
